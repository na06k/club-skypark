Wedding Hospitality

A wedding setting that feels grand without becoming complicated.

Club Skypark brings the core parts of a wedding together: elegant indoor function space, open-air celebration options, guest rooms, dining coordination and a reception team that can support family movement through the day.

For families, the biggest challenge is not only finding a beautiful place. It is managing arrival, rooms, food timings, rituals, elders, children, photography, dinner flow and late-night stays without confusion. This page is now kept text-first so visitors can understand exactly how the venue supports the celebration instead of only looking at pictures.

The venue can support wedding receptions, engagement ceremonies, ring functions, haldi-style daytime gatherings, family dinners and group stays. The hotel setting makes it easier to coordinate guests who are travelling from outside Neemuch because rooms, lobby support and dining are available within the same property.
